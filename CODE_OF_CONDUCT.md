# Code of Conduct

GitRoll is a welcoming project. Everyone who participates, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else, is expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep discussion focused on the work.
- Accept that decisions are sometimes "no", and disagree constructively.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing others' private information are not tolerated.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block people who don't follow these expectations. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainers through the repository's security contact described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
